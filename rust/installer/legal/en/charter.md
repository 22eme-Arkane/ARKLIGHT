# ARKLIGHT Charter

*Version 2, October 30, 2026 (ARKLIGHT 1.0)*

ARKLIGHT is a free lighting simulator, made to practise without any gear: you place fixtures
in a 3D scene, set them with their on-board menus, control them from a console, an app or a
MIDI controller, and prepare the lighting plot. This charter sets out the spirit in which we
use it. It goes with the terms of use.

## 1. The spirit of ARKLIGHT

- **Learn with confidence.** ARKLIGHT is a training ground: you can try anything, make
  mistakes and start again, with no risk to the gear or to anyone.
- **Your work is yours.** Your projects, lighting plots and settings stay on your computer.
  There is no account and no subscription.
- **Free and transparent.** The code is open (Apache-2.0 licence): anyone can read it, check it
  and improve it.
- **Nothing is set in stone.** This charter will evolve with the people who keep ARKLIGHT
  alive.

## 2. On a real set

- **A simulator is still a simulator.** ARKLIGHT's menus, colours, outputs and photometry are
  reproductions, sometimes approximate. When in doubt, the manufacturer's manual and the
  crew's instructions prevail.
- **Safety cannot be simulated.** Stand loads, rigging, slings, electrical connections, the
  heat of sources: ARKLIGHT gives guidance, never approval. On a set, follow the safety rules
  and the people in charge of them.
- **Addresses and patches** prepared in ARKLIGHT must be checked on the real gear before the
  shoot or the show.

## 3. Network and Bluetooth

- ARKLIGHT listens to Art-Net and sACN, and its virtual fixtures answer RDM like real
  devices: consoles and apps can discover and readdress them. **Do not connect it to the
  network of a running show or shoot** without the agreement of the person in charge:
  unexpected devices can cause confusion.
- Bluetooth mode makes your computer pose as a lighting device, so you can practise with an
  app. You agree not to use it to deceive or disturb other people's gear or apps.

## 4. The AI assistant and AIODOS

- ARKLIGHT's assistant uses **AIODOS**, ARKANE's local AI, by default: your requests, the
  state of your project and attached photos are computed on your computer. ARKLIGHT only
  connects to it after you agree in AIODOS (a code to compare), and access can be withdrawn in
  one click.
- AIODOS is separate software with its own charter: once installed, it may lend part of your
  computer's power to the public network, according to its settings. You choose whether to
  install it and how to set it up.
- You can also choose Ollama, or an online service with your own key: its terms then apply to
  what you send it.

## 5. Brands and manufacturers

- The names of fixtures, devices and apps (Astera, ARRI, Aputure, Blackout, Luminair…)
  belong to their owners. ARKLIGHT is neither affiliated with nor endorsed by these
  manufacturers.
- You agree not to present ARKLIGHT, its screenshots or its projects as an official product,
  tool or document of a manufacturer.

## 6. Respect for others

- Share your projects and lighting plots freely, but respect other people's work: plots,
  location photos or background images that are not yours are only shared with their
  author's consent.
- A lighting plot drawn by someone else (the photo of a paper plan that the assistant
  rebuilds) remains their work: ask for their consent before sharing it.
- The AI assistant acts on your behalf in your project: what you ask of it is your
  responsibility, just like what you do yourself.

## 7. Reporting

An error in a menu, a wrong DMX profile, a bug or a security flaw can be reported to the
project team, on its repository: github.com/22eme-Arkane/ARKLIGHT.
