## 4. What ARKLIGHT contacts

- **GitHub**, at start-up, to find out whether a new version has been published, and, when
  you click "Download and install", to download that version's installer. Nothing else is
  sent.
- **Your local network**, to receive Art-Net and sACN and to answer RDM (UDP ports 6454 and
  5568).
- **Your computer's Bluetooth**, only when you ask for it in the Bluetooth tab.
- For the AI assistant, **the service you choose**: AIODOS (offered by default) or Ollama,
  which run on your computer, or an online service (Anthropic, OpenAI, Mistral, Google) if you
  give it a key. Your request, what the assistant reads from your project and the photos you
  attach (a drawn plan, a location scout) are then sent to that service, under its own terms.
  Keys are encrypted on your computer.
- **Your computer itself**: ARKLIGHT listens on a local port (127.0.0.1, invisible from the
  network) so that only one window opens and projects launched later open in it.
- **ORAKLE**, ARKANE's dictation app, when you click a microphone or talk to the assistant:
  your voice stays on your computer.

nor a manufacturer's manual, nor the safety rules of a set.
